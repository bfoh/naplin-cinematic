Review every generation for distorted hands, uncanny faces, hairline artifacts, false claims, text artifacts and inconsistent grade. Do not use imagery without appropriate rights. Record provenance and date for each file.

## Polishing of the three supplied images (`scripts/polish-supplied.py`)

- **Curls portrait:** ~600 px of isolated white specks in the hair healed from a local median; deep-shadow blue cast neutralised toward ink; light unsharp mask. No face or skin retouching.
- **Hair macro:** restrained split-tone (warmer highlights, wine-ink shadows), slightly deeper blacks, light sharpening.
- **Crimson portrait:** only the saturated red backdrop (hue 350–12°, high saturation, soft-feathered mask) is darkened and shifted toward crimson-wine to match the palette. Skin, lips, hair and hand are untouched; checked at 1:1 for halos.

## Logo presentation

The official logo's crimson-to-plum gradient was designed for light grounds; on ink its purple half and the end of the wordmark disappear. An ivory label was used first; on 2026-10-17 the owner directed that the logo sit directly on the dark hero. `scripts/build-media.mjs` therefore derives `src/media/brand/hair-by-naplin-logo-on-dark.png` from the trimmed official file: only pixels darker than 40% lightness are lifted toward that floor (saturation eased by up to 18% where lifted). Hue, the crimson end, shapes and alpha are unchanged, and the original file is untouched in `assets/brand/`. It is used in the header, menu, footer and share image. This is a derived variant awaiting the owner's sign-off; an official on-dark or horizontal lockup from the brand's designer would supersede it (`MEDIA_TODO.md`).

## Known limits

- Masters are 1024–1536 px on the long edge. Full-bleed use is designed around this (aperture campaign, cutout hero, framed portraits); higher-resolution replacements would improve large desktop displays.
- The Closure Wig slot uses a styling portrait (labelled), not a closure-wig product photo. The salon has no service-specific imagery (pedicure, retouching, pony styles); services are presented typographically.
- No authentic premises, team, client or class photography exists yet; the story chapter is typographic by design.
